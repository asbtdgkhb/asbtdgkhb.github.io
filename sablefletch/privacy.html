1. The scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the developer of ArrowRush ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in the ArrowRush mobile application (the "App") and when you contact us about it. It is written for the current release and should be read together with the App Store privacy disclosures.

2. The information used by the game

ArrowRush is designed as a self-contained arrow-dash game. Core play does not require you to create an account or submit your name, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, camera, health information, or payment-card details.

The App may save game state and preferences on your device, such as unlocked rounds, best runs, control settings, sound choices, and tutorial status. This local information is used to restore your play and is not ordinarily sent to us.

3. Messages to player support

If you email us, we receive the address used to send the message and the content you choose to provide. A support request may include your device model, operating-system version, App version, screenshots, and a description of a problem. We use this information to reply, diagnose faults, improve reliability, and protect the App. Please do not send sensitive information unless it is relevant to the request.

4. Apple services and transactions

Apple may process information when you download, update, purchase, or restore the App through the App Store. If your device backup includes App data, Apple’s backup and account controls apply. Apple’s own privacy notice and terms govern those services. We do not receive your Apple ID or complete payment-card number from an App Store transaction.

If the App later adds an optional Apple service, such as Game Center, the relevant Apple account settings and any additional notice will apply before that feature is introduced.

5. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current App is intended to operate without cross-app advertising tracking. We do not use locally stored runs or arrow-control settings to build an advertising profile. If a future version adds an analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or similar SDK that collects information, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated to identify the data, purpose, sharing, and available controls.

6. Disclosure and service providers

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may disclose limited information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or a valid legal process, investigate abuse or fraud, protect users or our rights and systems, or complete a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets. A service provider that handles support or infrastructure information must use it only for the service we authorize and provide protection consistent with this policy and applicable law.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until you delete it, remove the App, reset its data, or remove a relevant device backup. Support messages are retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain a record of the issue, resolve disputes, improve security, or comply with a legal obligation.

To request deletion of support information, email asbtdgkhb@proton.me. We may ask for enough detail to verify the request and may retain information that law requires us to keep.

8. Security, children, and your choices

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the limited information we handle, but no electronic system is completely secure. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through ordinary gameplay. You can manage device permissions, backup, and local data through your device and App controls. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information we hold.

9. Updates to this policy

We may revise this policy when the App, our practices, or legal requirements change. The revised version will show a new date above and will be made available through an accessible App or website link. If a change materially affects personal-information handling, we will provide additional notice where required.
