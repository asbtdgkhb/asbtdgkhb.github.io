The developer of Sablefletch (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This notice explains what information is involved when you use the Sablefletch app (the “App”), why it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information used in the App

Sablefletch is designed to provide its core experience without asking you to submit a name, telephone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, camera, health information, or payment-card details. Core use does not require a player account.

The App may store information locally on your device, such as selected settings, completed content, current state, and related preferences. This local record lets the App remember your choices and resume play. It is not ordinarily transmitted to us during use.

2. Information you voluntarily send

When you contact us at the email address below, we receive your email address and the material you choose to send. This might include device or App-version information, screenshots, and details of a technical problem. We use support correspondence to respond, investigate, maintain the App, and protect against abuse or security problems. Please do not include sensitive information unless it is necessary to your question.

3. Apple and device-level services

Apple may process information when you download, update, restore, or purchase through the App Store. Your device may also include App data in an iCloud backup if you enable that service. These matters are governed by Apple’s policies and your device settings. We do not receive your Apple ID or full payment-card information from Apple.

If a future version provides an optional Apple-linked feature, its use will follow the relevant Apple controls and we will update this notice before a material change to the data practices takes effect.

4. Tracking, analytics, and advertising

The current App is not intended to conduct cross-app tracking or use your local App activity to create an advertising profile. If a later version adds a third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or similar tool that collects information, we will update this notice and the App Store privacy disclosures to identify the information, purpose, sharing, retention, and applicable user controls.

5. When information may be disclosed

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may disclose limited information if reasonably necessary to comply with law or a valid legal request; protect the safety, rights, security, or property of users, us, or others; investigate fraud or misuse; or complete a merger, financing, acquisition, or sale. We may also use or disclose aggregated or de-identified information that does not reasonably identify you.

If a service provider handles information for us, it may use that information only to provide the authorized service or as required by law, and must provide protection consistent with this notice and applicable law.

6. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local App information remains on your device until you delete it, remove the App, reset it using available controls, or remove it from a relevant backup. Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, maintain appropriate records, resolve a dispute, improve security, or meet legal obligations.

Privacy requests. Depending on where you live, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of personal information we hold. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the limited information we handle, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support without appropriate authorization, please contact us.

8. Changes and contact

We may revise this notice when the App, its data practices, or legal requirements change. A revised notice will show a new issue date and will be made accessible through the App or a linked policy page. Where required, we will provide additional notice of material changes.
